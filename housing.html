Student Life / Housing & Accommodations

Housing & Accommodations

Metropolitan Community College is committed to ensuring a smooth transition for our students. While on-campus housing is not available, our Downtown Vancouver location is surrounded by a variety of off-campus housing options.

Homestay Student residences Apartment rentals
Finding your home in Vancouver

Off-campus Housing Options

Your ideal housing choice will depend on your budget, preferred level of independence, and the kind of living experience you want while studying in Canada.

Homestay

Staying with a Canadian family can be an enriching cultural experience and an important part of student life. Homestay is best suited to students who want affordable housing, a meal package, and an opportunity to learn about Canadian culture.

Students are introduced to carefully screened host families who welcome them as part of the household. Prices vary, and our Student Support team works closely with students and host families to help find a suitable match.

Student Residences

Students may choose a residence operated by a third-party provider. These modern, fully furnished accommodations are designed with comfort, safety, and convenience in mind, creating a supportive living experience throughout your studies in Canada.

Student residences are an upscale, worry-free option for students seeking a convenient and supportive place to live.

Apartment Rental

Students with strong communication skills may choose to rent an apartment independently or share with others. Rental availability, costs, and requirements vary by landlord.

We usually do not encourage new students to rent an apartment directly. If this is the option you prefer, speak with our Student Support team and we will do our best to help.

Housing support from MCC

We are here to help you compare your options

Our Student Support team can help you select an appropriate housing option while studying at Metropolitan Community College. For assistance, please visit the front reception or email admin@metropolitancollege.ca.

Free assistance is available to prospective and current MCC students only. This support provides general information; students should seek separate legal advice when needed.

Know before you rent

Renter Rights and Responsibilities

It is important to know your rights as a renter and understand the responsibilities you and your landlord each have. Rental policies vary across Canada, so students living in British Columbia should become familiar with the provincial rules that apply to their tenancy.

Before you move in

Lease Agreement

Before moving into your accommodation, you and your landlord need to agree to the terms of your rental agreement. A written agreement is strongly encouraged.

British Columbia has laws that specifically relate to landlords and tenants. Make sure you understand your obligations as a renter and what your landlord is permitted to do.
